## [1.0.0]

### Added
- **RaidSort** Paste a group layout from the Raidnight website (plain text or an `RS1:` code) and the raid sorts itself into the planned groups. Includes a live preview, inviting players who are missing, and an option to fill open slots with players who are not on the list, by role. Open it with `/raidnight` or `/rn`.
- **Auto** Balances groups 1–4 by role: groups 1+2 and 3+4 each get the same share of tanks, healers, melee and ranged. Melee or ranged specs of Hunters, Shamans, Druids and Demon Hunters are inspected and kept up to date during the session.
- **Localization** The interface follows the language of the game client, with English as the fallback.
- **Boss layouts** A row of boss icons from the current raid tier at the top of the RaidSort window. Save a layout for each boss and load it again with one click. Optionally, every pull saves the raid's groups under that boss automatically.
